## 0.1.0

- Initial release, extracted from the DartPDF app: `DocumentTabs` (the
  tab-set model), `DocumentTabStrip` + `DocumentTabStripController` (the
  Chrome-style strip), `DocumentTabsOverview` + `DocumentTabTile` (the
  searchable, multi-select tabs grid), `DocumentTabsButton`,
  `DocumentTabDragStartListener`, `MiddleEllipsisText` and
  `compareTabTitles`.
- `DocumentTabStrip.activeTabColor` / `tabColor` override the tab fills.
