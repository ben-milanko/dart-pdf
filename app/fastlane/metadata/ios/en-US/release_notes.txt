New in 3.0.0

LANGUAGES
• DartPDF is now available in 10 languages, with full right-to-left layout for Arabic.

RENDERING
• More accurate overprint, blend-mode and soft-mask rendering, so print-oriented PDFs look closer to their intended output.
• Transparency groups drawn under a blend mode now composite correctly, fixing stray opaque boxes on some files.

EDITING
• Hold Shift while drawing to constrain ink and lines to straight strokes.
• Set a default style for annotations and reuse it with one tap.
• Redesigned document tabs that stay readable as you open more files.

SPEED
• Faster rendering and lower memory use, with the biggest gains on large or image-heavy files.

Thank you for using DartPDF. Feedback and bug reports are always welcome.
