New in 8.1.0

- Change the font, size, colour and alignment of dropdown and list-box form fields, including several selected fields together.
- Use the new phone-friendly menu to create, open, print and sign documents.
- Edit free text while keeping its original font, line wrapping and baseline. The caret stays aligned after resizing text.
- The iOS text-selection menu stays clear of the style controls, and inline text opens the keyboard more reliably in iOS browsers.
- The iOS share sheet shows the document filename.
- Choose from the full colour palette in the mobile pen and highlighter tune menu.
