New in 3.2.0

PERFORMANCE
• Page caching now adapts to available device memory and system pressure.
• The rendering pipeline avoids duplicate full-page and detail work, keeping large and visually dense PDFs responsive.

ANNOTATIONS
• Third-party FreeText boxes and callouts render more faithfully, including wrapping, alignment, fills, borders, and leader lines.
• Multi-line highlights, underlines, strikeouts, and squiggles select only where the markup is visible.

FIXES
• Loading and remote-session restoration stay responsive.
• Ctrl/Cmd-wheel zoom continues to work after drawing a constrained line.

Thank you for using DartPDF. Feedback and bug reports are always welcome.
