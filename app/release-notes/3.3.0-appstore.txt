New in 3.3.0

PERFORMANCE
• Pages you revisit can open instantly from full-resolution memory and disk caches.
• Optional idle warming prepares more pages before you navigate to them.
• Deep zoom is sharper on scanned and image-based PDFs because visible tiles reload the source image at the requested detail.

EDITING
• Touch panning responds sooner and works reliably from the canvas around a page.
• Rectangle corner radius is now available in the properties panel.
• Newly inserted PDF pages stay in view, and the floating toolbar no longer obscures the end of a document.

Thank you for using DartPDF. Feedback and bug reports are always welcome.
