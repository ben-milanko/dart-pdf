New in 9.0.0

- Group annotations so they move, resize, rotate, copy and paste as one. Rotate a selection around its shared centre or each item's own centre.
- Scanned documents are recognised automatically so their text can be selected and searched. You can turn this off in Settings. New scans are named after the date they were captured.
- Optionally save the page colour into saved and exported PDFs.
- Make selected text bold, italic or underlined with keyboard shortcuts, and style callout arrows separately from their text box.
- Draw polylines more easily: pan while placing, scroll at the edges and undo the last point. The page grid shows where a page will land and animates reordering.
- Long-press and drag to draw a selection box, and selection now responds as soon as you lift your finger.
- Documents that use unembedded serif fonts look closer to the original, text keeps a level baseline, and rotated images stay sharp when you zoom in.
- Fixes for documents showing another document's images, text recognition and sign-in for digital signatures, reopening recent files after an update, and first launch in unsupported languages.
- DartPDF now tells you when a recent document's file has moved.
- Smoother scrolling.
