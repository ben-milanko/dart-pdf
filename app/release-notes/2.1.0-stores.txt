New in 2.1.0

• Reflow reading view: read a document as flowing text, with lazy scrolling, a saved position and a figure viewer.
• Faster opening and rendering, especially on large or image-heavy files, and lower memory use on very wide drawings.
• Crisper hairlines, so fine linework stays legible when zoomed out.
• Better colour on gradients and on indexed images.
• Symbol and ZapfDingbats characters render instead of boxes.
