New in 3.1.0

SCAN AND CAPTURE
• Scan paper documents straight into a PDF — start a new document from a scan or insert pages into the open file.
• Take a photo with the camera and add it to your document.

PEACE OF MIND
• Unsaved changes are mirrored in the background and restored after a crash, so your work survives.

NEW TOOLS
• Add hyperlinks to the web or to another page in the document.
• Crop placed images and pasted pictures.
• Lock annotations against accidental edits, and unlock them when needed.
• Every editing tool now has a keyboard shortcut.
• Search now finds text inside your notes and annotations, too.

RENDERING
• Overprint now renders faithfully, so print-oriented PDFs with knockouts and spot inks look the way they print.
• Pages reveal progressively as they render — much faster on large or image-heavy files.
• Recent files can show as a grid of page thumbnails.
• Shrink a PDF's file size losslessly when saving.

Thank you for using DartPDF. Feedback and bug reports are always welcome.
