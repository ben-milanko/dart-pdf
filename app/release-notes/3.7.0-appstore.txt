New in 3.7.0

- Pages now render as you scroll past them instead of arriving after you stop, and pages you have already read come back instantly.
- Find and replace in the search panel: replace one match or every match in a single undo step.
- Fixed scrambled characters in the content tool on documents with subsetted fonts, which also made it edit the wrong text.
- Fixed "Replace text" changing every matching line on a page instead of the one selected.
- Fixed a crash when opening or saving a file.
