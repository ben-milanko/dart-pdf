# Fill and sign a PDF with DartPDF

You can fill a PDF form and add a drawn signature without uploading the
document to an online editor. DartPDF is a free PDF editor for Android,
iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux. You can also
[use it in your browser](https://app.dart-pdf.com).

[Install DartPDF for your device](https://dart-pdf.com/#download), then open
your PDF. The app does not require an account. Editing happens on your device;
sharing the finished file sends it to the destination you choose.

## Keep the original

Start with a copy of the PDF, especially if you need to keep the blank form.
On desktop, **Save** can overwrite the original file. Use **Save as** to give
the completed copy a different name, such as `completed-form.pdf`.

On Android, iPhone and iPad, open the PDF in DartPDF from its file picker or
your device's Share or Open With menu. The app's save and share controls use
your device's file destinations. In a browser, saving downloads a PDF; it
does not replace the file you opened.

## Fill a form that already has fields

A fillable PDF has interactive fields. A flat form has printed boxes and
blank lines but no fields to type into. A scanned form may be an image of
the whole page.

For an interactive form:

1. Choose the **Form fields** tool.
2. Select a field, then double-click or double-tap it to fill it.
3. Enter the text, select checkboxes or radio buttons, and choose values from
   any dropdowns.
4. Check the result before saving, including any calculated totals.

Dragging with the Form fields tool creates a new field rather than filling
an existing one. If you only want to answer the form, select its existing
fields instead.

Some forms depend on scripts or specialised XFA features. DartPDF supports
common PDF form helpers, not every form script. If a calculation does not
work or a form does not display correctly, use the software its issuer
recommends rather than sending an incomplete result.

## Write on a form without fields

If there is no field to fill, use **Text box** to place an answer over the
printed blank. Choose a readable font size and move or resize the box so
the answer fits without covering the question.

This works on scanned pages too. You do not need OCR just to place an answer
over a scan. OCR adds searchable and selectable text; it does not turn the
printed blanks into interactive form fields.

## Add a drawn signature

Choose **Signature** and draw your signature in the signature pad. Place it
on the signing line, then adjust its position and size.

This places a drawn mark on the page. It is different from a
certificate-backed digital signature, which can be checked for changes to
the signed document bytes. DartPDF has a separate **Digital signature** tool
for that workflow. Check which signing method the recipient requires;
placing a drawing does not guarantee that they will accept it.

## Save the completed PDF and check it

Save a copy to a location you can find. On mobile, choose a file destination
in the share sheet if you want to check it before sending. In a browser,
find the saved PDF through your browser's downloads list.

Reopen the saved file and check every page:

- Are the answers visible and inside the intended spaces?
- Are the correct boxes selected?
- Is the signature in the right place?
- Do calculated values look right?

For an important form, check the saved PDF in the viewer the recipient
normally uses too. Keep the editable copy until they confirm receipt.

## Get DartPDF

[Download DartPDF](https://dart-pdf.com/#download) for your phone, tablet or
computer, or [open the browser version](https://app.dart-pdf.com). The download
page links to the official app stores and desktop builds.

DartPDF is free and open source. You can fill the form locally without an
account, ads or a subscription.
